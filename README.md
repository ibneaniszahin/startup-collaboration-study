# Bangladesh Startup Ecosystem Collaboration Study

An explorable version of my peer-reviewed study, **"Startup support in emerging
economies: a study of collaboration among government agencies in Bangladesh"**
(*Asia Pacific Journal of Innovation and Entrepreneurship*, Emerald, 2026 — open
access, CC BY 4.0).

The study finds that the binding constraint on Bangladesh's startup ecosystem is
not capital but coordination failure inside the public sector. This page turns
its findings into an interactive read: a scroll-driven redrawing of the study's
collaboration map (built with USAID's collaboration mapping instrument), the
cultural mechanisms behind it, and the paper's proposals — explorable in minutes
instead of eighteen pages.

**Live page:** https://ibneaniszahin.github.io/startup-coordination-study/
**Full paper (DOI):** https://doi.org/10.1108/APJIE-06-2024-0140

Fieldwork 2024 · 15 organizations · mixed-methods (interviews, document analysis,
collaboration mapping survey). The 2024 reading is a baseline; the diagnostic is
public and replicable — contact: zahin.ibneanis@gmail.com

Single self-contained `index.html` (no build, no dependencies) + `og-image.png`.
